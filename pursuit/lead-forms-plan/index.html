Club Launch Engine

How every lead reaches GymSales

One setup for every club. A lead from any form lands in the right club's GymSales, with its source and tags, ready for the sales team to call.

Three ways a lead comes in

WebsiteThe free trial form on pursuitmuaythai.com. Connected for every club.
Meta lead adsForms filled in on Facebook and Instagram. Connected for every club.
Landing pagesCampaign pages for a club or for the whole network. One new connection, built once and now live.

What happens when a new club opens

  • The club's GymSales account is created.
  • We add the club to the website form, the Meta form and the connection to GymSales.
  • From then on, any landing page or campaign for that club sends its leads straight to that club's GymSales. Nothing is rebuilt.

What this gives us

  • A new club's forms are ready the same day its GymSales account exists. Armstrong Creek's were.
  • Every lead carries where it came from and which campaign, so the report shows what each stage brought in.
  • National campaigns and single-club campaigns run on the same setup.
  • Job ad applications are kept out of the sales leads.
