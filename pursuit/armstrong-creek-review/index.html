Pursuit Armstrong Creek: launch review

The pages and ads for the first two stages of the launch, in one place. Start on the first tab.

What is here

  • Voucher page: where every awareness ad sends people, from Tue 1 Dec.
  • Thank-you page: what they see after claiming.
  • Competition pages: a draft. The dates and terms are not set, so they show as to be confirmed.
  • Awareness ads: 10 ads, 1 to 13 Dec. Voucher only, no prices.
  • Stage 1 ads: 16 ads, 14 Dec to 3 Jan. The founding member offer.

How to give feedback

  • Every ad has a code in blue (AW1, S1-01, FC1). Use the code when you talk about an ad.
  • Each ad is shown in the three shapes it runs in: feed (4:5), square (1:1) and story (9:16). Click any image for the full size.
  • For the pages, say which section you mean (the heading is enough).
  • You can fill in the forms on this page. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Worth knowing

  • The photos are real members and coaches at other Pursuit clubs. Armstrong Creek photos replace them after the first shoot.
  • The reviews are real Google reviews from other Pursuit clubs, quoted word for word.
  • Nothing here is live. No ad is in Meta and no page is published.
