01 The funnel

Six stages, from first awareness to an open club

Every club launch runs through the same six stages. Everything in this project is built to fit them.

1

Awareness

Pursuit is coming. Locals register for early bird before any offer is live.

2

Competition

The viral comp. Entries and referral sharing build the lead list fast.

3

Presale phase 1

Early bird rates open to the list and the local area.

4

Presale phase 2

The second offer, for everyone who missed phase 1.

5

Presale phase 3

Last chance. Countdown to opening and the final call on presale pricing.

6

Open, business as usual

Early bird is retired. The always-on offer takes over: the free 7-day pass.

03 How it gets built

Built once, so it never gets rebuilt

The assets are the first half of the job. Turning them into an engine is the second.

Every detail locked

Each asset is worked through with a fine-tooth comb until it is right and can be replicated for any club, in any state.

  • Every section on every page
  • The tone of voice, line by line
  • Every pixel of each static
  • Where every headline sits
  • The imagery and how it is treated
  • How each animation runs
  • Club name, suburb and address
  • Offer and opening date

Turned into an engine Pursuit owns

Once every asset is locked, each one becomes a reference and a custom skill inside Claude Code. Those skills are bundled into master skills that produce a whole club launch, pages, statics, animations, emails and Google Ads, in a single run.

The skills, templates and references are built for Pursuit and owned by Pursuit. The next club is a new name and address going through the same engine, not a new project.
