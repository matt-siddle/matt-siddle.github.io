Packing guide: shorts now, gear in December

Gloves and shin guards ordered in the sale (16 to 30 Nov) ship in December, when the new stock lands. Everything else ships now. Updated Mon 16 Nov, 2pm: Fight & Flex tees and paid wraps now ship straight away.
  • Order with gear and other items: one at a time, set the gear to 0 in the box, then generate.
  • Fight & Flex bundle: treat it as a mixed order. The tee ships now, the gloves stay on hold.
  • Wraps the customer paid for ship now. The free gift wraps go in whatever parcel ships first.
  • Order that is only gear: no label. Leave it.
  • Everything else: exactly the same as normal.

01 Mixed orders

An order with gear and other items

Shorts, tees or paid wraps in the same order as gloves or shin guards, and every Fight & Flex bundle (its tee ships now, its gloves wait). The other items ship now, with the free gift wraps if the order has them.

1

Spot the order

It has an On hold section in Shopify with the note "Black Friday pre-order: gear ships December". The gloves or shin guards sit in that section; everything else sits under Unfulfilled.

2

Open the label screen for that one order

More actions, then AU Post Generate Label. Do these one at a time, not in the bulk run. The box will list everything, the gear included.

3

Take the gear out of the box

Click Edit on the box. Set the gloves or shin guards to 0, leave everything else as it is, then Save.

4

Check it, then generate

The gear now shows as Unpacked and the box holds the rest. Click Generate Label.

5

Click Ok on the warning

It asks "Unpacked items exists. Do you want to continue?" Click Ok.

6

Done

Print and pack as normal. The customer gets a tracking email for what you sent. The gear stays on hold on the order until December.

02 Gear only

An order that is only gear

Nothing ships until December.

1

Leave it

If an order is only gloves, shin guards or a Sentinel Set, do not generate a label. That includes orders where the only other item is the free gift wraps (the line marked "Black Friday free gift"). The whole order is on hold and ships in December.

2

Keep it out of the bulk run

When you select orders for the bulk label run, leave out anything with an On hold section.

03 On the shelf

Gear we already have

Some gear is here now and can go out.

1

Pink and All Black gloves go out as normal

Pastel Pink and All Black gloves are only shelf stock, so they are not put on hold, on their own or in a Fight & Flex bundle. Label them like any other order. In a Sentinel Set they wait with the shin guards.

2

Anything else that is on the shelf

The hold assumes no gear is going out. If the item is physically there, send it: on the order in Shopify click Release hold, then generate the label as normal with everything in the box.

04 December

When the new stock lands

For later. Nothing to do here during the sale.

1

Release the hold

When the new stock lands, open the order in Shopify and click Release hold. The gear moves to Unfulfilled.

2

Orders where something already shipped

Use Shopify's own button, Create shipping label, on the gear. The AU Post app will not make a second label for an order it has already labelled.

3

Pick the package and buy

Choose the package that matches (Gloves, Sentinel Shin Guards, Sentinel Set Packaging), check the weight, then Buy shipping label. The customer gets a second tracking email.

4

Orders that were gear only

Nothing has shipped on these, so label them the usual way in the AU Post app.

Anything that looks wrong, message Matt before generating the label.
