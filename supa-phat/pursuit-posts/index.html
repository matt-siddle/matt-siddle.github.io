20 posts for the Pursuit pages

Organic posts in Pursuit's branding that put Supa Phat in front of members at the moment they need gear. Each one is pinned to something that happens to a member, so it reads as a Pursuit post.

  • The member perk is 5% off at supa-phat.com. The code itself is never on a post or in a caption, so it cannot be scraped and passed around: members get it at the front desk and by text.
  • Posts marked with a time of year sit in that month; the rest go anywhere in the calendar, about 2 a month.
  • Every post tags @supaphatfg, and the caption sends people to supa-phat.com. There are no buttons on the tiles because an organic post cannot be tapped through.
  • Where a post suits a text message, the text is written underneath it.
  • Posts 08, 10 and 11 are templates: a club swaps in its own member photo, coach or suburb.
